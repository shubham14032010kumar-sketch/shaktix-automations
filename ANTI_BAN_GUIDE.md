# 🛡️ Shaktix WhatsApp Anti-Ban & Safe Sending Guide

WhatsApp par bulk marketing karte waqt number ban hona 100% preventable (rokne yogya) hai agar aap in rules aur protocols ko follow karte hain. Yeh guide aapke aur aapke clients ke numbers ko hamesha safe rakhegi.

---

## 🚦 Rule #1: The 7-Day Number Warm-Up Schedule
Agar aapne naya SIM card liya hai ya kisi naye number se pehli baar WhatsApp marketing shuru kar rahe hain, toh **pehle hi din 500 messages kabhi mat bhejo**. WhatsApp ke AI algorithms naye numbers ki activity ko monitor karte hain.

### Warm-Up Timetable:
| Din (Day) | Daily Sending Limit | Message Type / Target Audience | Recommended Delay |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Day 1** | **20 – 30 messages** | Dosto, parichit logo ya regular customers ko (jinka reply aaye) | 25 – 40 seconds |
| **Day 2** | **40 – 50 messages** | Mix: 50% puraane contacts + 50% naye leads | 20 – 35 seconds |
| **Day 3** | **70 – 100 messages** | Naye leads jinme personalized name `{Name}` ho | 20 – 30 seconds |
| **Day 4** | **120 – 150 messages** | Bulk campaign (offer / intro message) | 15 – 25 seconds |
| **Day 5** | **200 – 250 messages** | Targeted local businesses | 15 – 25 seconds |
| **Day 6** | **300 – 400 messages** | Normal active campaign | 15 – 20 seconds |
| **Day 7+** | **500+ messages** | Full capacity campaigns | 12 – 20 seconds |

> **Incoming Replies Matter!** WhatsApp un numbers ko safe maanta hai jinke messages par saamne se **Replies (Jawab)** aate hain. Shuruat me hamesha aisa question poocho jisse log reply karein (Jaise: *"Kya aap interested hain? Reply YES for Demo"*).

---

## ⏱️ Rule #2: Delay Intervals (Kabhi Zero Delay Mat Rakho)
WhatsApp human behavior ko track karta hai. Koi bhi insaan 1 second me 5 messages nahi bhej sakta.

* **Minimum Delay:** `15 seconds`
* **Maximum Delay:** `25 seconds`
* **Randomized Sleep:** Har 40-50 messages ke baad tool ko 2-3 minute ka pause (rest) lene dein. Shaktix Bulk Sender me yeh random sleep feature built-in hai.

---

## 📝 Rule #3: Message Spintax & Personalization
Agar aap ek hi exact message 500 logo ko bhejenge, toh WhatsApp ka spam filter use detect kar lega.

### 1. Dynamic Tags Ka Use Karein:
Hamesha customer ka naam ya business name lagayein:
* ❌ Galat: *"Namaste, humare paas aapke liye website offer hai..."*
* ✅ Sahi: *"Namaste **{Name} ji**, humne dekha aapka **{Business}** ka kaam bohot badhiya hai..."*

### 2. Message Variations (Spintax / Greetings):
Har message me thoda farak hona chahiye:
* Variation A: *"Namaste {Name} ji,"*
* Variation B: *"Hello {Name},"*
* Variation C: *"Good afternoon {Name} sir,"*

---

## 🚫 Rule #4: Spamming Se Bachein (Consent & Unsubscribe Option)
Kisi ko tang mat karein. Message ke end me ek polite opt-out line zaroor likhein:
> *"Agar aap dobara message nahi chahte toh kripya 'STOP' likh dein, hum aapko dubara message nahi karenge."*

Jab log message ko **"Report as Spam"** ya **"Block"** karte hain, tabhi number ban hota hai. Agar aapne STOP ka option diya hai, toh log report karne ke bajay STOP likh denge, aur aapka number 100% safe rahega!

---

## 🔄 Agar Number Ban Ho Jaye Toh 24 Ghante Mein Unban Kaise Karein?
Agar galti se temporary ban lag bhi jaye, toh ghabraiye nahi:
1. WhatsApp open karein aur **"Request a Review"** par click karein.
2. Review box me yeh professional message paste karein:
   > *"Hello WhatsApp Support Team, my WhatsApp account was flagged by mistake while communicating with my regular business clients. I strictly follow WhatsApp terms of service and do not spam. Kindly review and restore my account as soon as possible. Thank you."*
3. **90% cases me 6 se 24 ghante ke andar number restore ho jata hai.**
