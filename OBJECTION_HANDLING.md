# 🎯 Shaktix Client Objection Handling Playbook

Jab aap local dukaandaro, coaching walo ya clinic owners ko call ya message karenge, toh 90% log aapse wahi 4-5 sawaal karenge. Agar aapne yeh exact jawab de diye, toh deal turant close hogi!

---

### Objection #1: "Bohot mehenga hai, thoda saste me karo."

#### ❌ Galat Jawab:
*"Theek hai sir, aap ₹500 kam de dena."* (Isse client sochta hai aapka kaam cheap quality ka hai).

#### ✅ Sahi Counter Script (The ROI Angle):
> *"Sir, main aapki baat samajh sakta hoon. Lekin ek baat sochiye—agar aap newspaper me ad dete hain ya pamplet bantwate hain, toh ₹4,000–₹5,000 ek hi din me kharch ho jaate hain aur log pamplet fek dete hain.*  
> *Hamare WhatsApp Bulk Sender se aap daily 500-1000 logo ke personal phone me apna offer bhej sakte hain. Agar pure mahine me isse sirf 2 se 3 naye customer bhi aa gaye, toh aapka poora software ka paisa pehle hi hafte me recover ho jayega!*  
> *Aur ye one-time investment hai, daily ka kharcha nahi. Kya hum aaj aapke liye 50 messages ka free test demo setup karein?"*

---

### Objection #2: "WhatsApp number ban toh nahi hoga?"

#### ❌ Galat Jawab:
*"Nahi sir, bilkul nahi hoga."* (Over-promise lagta hai).

#### ✅ Sahi Counter Script (The Expert Safety Angle):
> *"Sir, bilkul sahi sawaal poocha aapne! Log wahan fasate hain jahan wo kisi bekar software se 1 second me 500 message bhej dete hain aur WhatsApp unka number block kar deta hai.*  
> *Hamare Shaktix software me **Anti-Ban Human Delay Engine** aur **Random Sleep Technology** built-in hai. Yeh software har message ke beech 15 se 25 second ka randomized gap leta hai, jaise koi insaan khud haath se type karke bhej raha ho.*  
> *Saath hi hum aapko apna **7-Day Safe Warm-up Protocol** dete hain jisse aapka number 100% safe rehta hai. Hum pehle aapke samne 20 messages bhej kar dikhayenge, jab aap satisfied ho jayein tab aage badhiyega."*

---

### Objection #3: "Hamare paas pehle se website ya banda hai."

#### ❌ Galat Jawab:
*"Acha theek hai sir, jab zaroorat ho batana."* (Lead khatam).

#### ✅ Sahi Counter Script (The Performance Audit & Upgrade Angle):
> *"Bohot badhiya sir! Iska matlab aap technology ki value samajhte hain.*  
> *Sir, hamari team ne aapki current website dekhi thi—wo mobile par thoda slow load hoti hai aur usme direct WhatsApp lead capture button nahi hai. Aaj kal 95% log mobile se website kholte hain.*  
> *Aur doosri baat—sirf website hone se customer nahi aate, jab tak aapke paas un tak pahunchne ka marketing tool na ho. Hamara WhatsApp Bulk Sender aapki existing website par daily hazaron visitors drive kar sakta hai.*  
> *Main aapke current setup ke sath ek chhota audit report share karta hoon, 2 minute lagega dekhne me."*

---

### Objection #4: "Hume zaroorat nahi hai, hamara business theek chal raha hai."

#### ✅ Sahi Counter Script:
> *"Sir, business acha chal raha hai yeh bohot khushi ki baat hai! Lekin sochiye, aapke pure area me jitne naye log shift ho rahe hain ya aapke competitors marketing kar rahe hain, unka kya?*  
> *Agar aap apne existing 500 puraane customers ko har festival ya mahine me ek baar WhatsApp par naya offer ya wishes bhejte hain, toh aapka repeat business 30% badh jayega bina kisi extra advertising cost ke.*  
> *Sirf 10 minute ka live demo dekhiye, agar pasand na aaye toh bilkul mat lijiye!"*

---

### Objection #5: "Quotation WhatsApp par bhej do, dekh ke batata hoon."

#### ✅ Sahi Action & Script:
> *"Bilkul sir! Main aapko Shaktix ka 1-page official proposal WhatsApp kar raha hoon. Isme hamare 3 plans hain—Starter, Growth Combo, aur Custom.*  
> *Sir, main shaam ko 5 baje ek baar call karunga taaki aapka koi sawaal ho toh clear kar saku. 5 baje call karna convenient rahega na sir?"*  
> *(Call ka time turant lock karein taaki follow-up aasan ho jaye).*
